# Geododecagon Model of Intelligence

A theoretical multidimensional framework describing human intelligence as an integrated system of twelve interacting cognitive, emotional, and behavioral domains.

---

## Overview

The **Geododecagon Model of Intelligence** proposes that intelligence is not a single measurable quantity but rather a dynamic structure composed of multiple interacting domains.  

The model conceptualizes intelligence as a **geometric structure resembling a dodecagon (12-sided polygon)**, where each vertex represents a distinct domain of intelligence and each edge represents the interaction between domains.

This framework attempts to integrate ideas from cognitive psychology, neuroscience, philosophy of mind, and educational theory.

---

## Core Principles

The model is built upon three central principles.

### 1. Multidimensionality
Human intelligence consists of multiple cognitive and behavioral capacities rather than a single general factor.

### 2. Interconnectivity
The domains of intelligence interact with one another to produce complex intellectual behavior.

### 3. Dynamic Balance
Optimal intellectual functioning arises when the different domains operate in a balanced configuration.

---

## The Twelve Domains of Intelligence

The Geododecagon Model identifies twelve domains.

| Domain | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Cognitive Intelligence | Analytical reasoning, logic, and problem-solving |
| Metacognitive Intelligence | Awareness and regulation of thinking processes |
| Creative Intelligence | Generation of novel ideas and innovative thinking |
| Intuitive Intelligence | Rapid pattern recognition and subconscious inference |
| Sensory-Perceptual Intelligence | Interpretation of sensory information |
| Bodily-Kinesthetic Intelligence | Physical coordination and motor control |
| Practical Intelligence | Application of knowledge in real-world contexts |
| Adaptive Intelligence | Adjustment to environmental change |
| Social Intelligence | Understanding social dynamics and interpersonal relations |
| Emotional Intelligence | Recognition and regulation of emotions |
| Self-Awareness Intelligence | Reflective understanding of one's mental state |
| Moral Intelligence | Ethical reasoning and moral evaluation |

---

## Structural Interaction

The model emphasizes that intelligence emerges from **interactions between domains**, rather than isolated abilities.

Example interactions:

Creative Intelligence  
= Cognitive Intelligence + Intuitive Intelligence

Social Intelligence  
= Emotional Intelligence + Perceptual Awareness

Adaptive Intelligence  
= Cognitive Intelligence + Practical Intelligence + Emotional Regulation

These interactions create a **networked structure of intelligence**.
